About This System

The Flood Monitoring System for Barangay Bayugo provides real-time water level readings, live camera feeds, and automated alerts to help residents stay informed and safe during heavy rains and flood-prone conditions.

Sensors are placed at two key points: the Bayugo River bridge and Poblacion Creek. When water levels cross set thresholds, the system raises the alert level and can notify residents by SMS.

Built with care for the community, by the community.
